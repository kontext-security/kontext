The contextual DeBERTa-v3-xsmall base is Microsoft MIT-licensed (see LICENSE in this directory). Kontext's fine-tuned checkpoint is combined with two independently trained sparse heads. The request-intent head is adapted from MASSIVE 1.1 en-US, Copyright Amazon.com Inc. or its affiliates, derived from SLURP, licensed CC BY 4.0: https://github.com/alexa/massive and https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Adaptations include a binary query-intent mapping, deduplication, TF-IDF feature extraction and fitted logistic weights. No raw MASSIVE utterances or local captures are included in this serving bundle.

The contextual checkpoint was fine-tuned on TS-Bench, from "ToolSafe: Enhancing Tool Invocation Safety of LLM-based Agents via Proactive Step-level Guardrail and Feedback" by Yutao Mou, Zhangchi Xue, Lijun Li, Peiyang Liu, Shikun Zhang, Wei Ye and Jing Shao, licensed Apache-2.0: https://huggingface.co/datasets/MurrayTom/TS-Bench and https://www.apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0 . TS-Bench training data is derived from AgentAlign (Apache-2.0, https://huggingface.co/datasets/jc-ryan/AgentAlign) and ASB (MIT, https://github.com/agiresearch/ASB). No raw TS-Bench examples are included in this serving bundle.
